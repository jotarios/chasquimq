Pipelined acknowledgement

Acking one job at a time is the bottleneck most Redis-backed queues never escape. ChasquiMQ batches up to 64 acks per round trip and uses Redis 8.2's XACKDEL to acknowledge and delete in the same call.

The engine collects job IDs as workers finish, holds them in a per-consumer ring with a 5ms flush deadline, and ships them in a single pipeline command. On a contended Apple M3 host against redis:8.6.2, this lifts bulk produce to 211,074 jobs/s — 3.47× the Node.js baseline measured on the same hardware.

The trade-off is a small worst-case lag between job completion and ack visibility (≤5ms). For at-least-once semantics this is invisible; for at-most-once delivery you want the idempotent producer (XADD ... IDMPAUTO, Redis 8.6) on the enqueue side.
